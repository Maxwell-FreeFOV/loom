# Reading tool: my requirements (notes)

- I read articles and PDFs, and I want to save excerpts together with where they came from (page, section, link).
- Finding an excerpt later matters more to me than organizing it perfectly.
- It's just me — no accounts, no multi-user anything.
- Sync across devices would be nice, but not at the cost of running a server.
- I want to own the files: plain text I can grep, not a proprietary database.
