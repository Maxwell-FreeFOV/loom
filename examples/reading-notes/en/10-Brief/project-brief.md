# Project Brief: reading-notes-demo

<!-- Demo data for Loom's documentation example; not a real project. -->

> A personal tool for saving and finding reading excerpts.

## Problem to solve
I read articles and PDFs and lose the passages worth keeping. I want to save excerpts with their source and find them again later.

## Goals and success criteria
- v1 is in daily use within a few weekends.
- Finding a saved excerpt takes seconds.

## Scope
- In scope: saving excerpts locally with their source; searching them locally. See [[v1-scope]].
- Out of scope: accounts, sharing, multi-device sync (deferred by [[DR-2026-001_v1-local-excerpts]]).

## Constraints
- Time: weekends only.
- Resources: one person; no server budget.
- Technical: files stay plain text that I own.

## Key assumptions (to be validated)
- Plain-text search stays good enough at a few hundred excerpts.

## Stakeholders
- Just me.
